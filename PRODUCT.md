# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Israelis during rocket, UAV, and infiltration events. They often check from a stairwell or shelter, under stress. They want to know: is it my area, are my people OK, can I leave?
- Journalists and news sites that embed the live map (`/embed`).
- Broadcasters who use the OBS view (`/broadcast`).
- Phone and desktop get equal design attention.

## Product Purpose

ClearMap (מפה שקופה) gives the fastest, clearest live picture of Home Front Command (פיקוד העורף) alerts on a map. On top of that it answers personal questions: my zones, my people, and the official "event ended".

## Positioning

Red Alert-style apps just list cities. ClearMap shows the shape of the event on a map and follows the people you care about. It says "you can leave the shelter" only after an official Oref clear, never just because a zone timed out.

## Operating Context

Alerts arrive in bursts. A salvo can light up hundreds of zones in seconds. The flow is early warning (pre_alert), then red alert, then stay in the shelter (after_alert), then the official "event ended" clear. UAV and terrorist-infiltration events have their own states. People use the app in a dark shelter at night and outdoors in sunlight.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hebrew-first, RTL.
- Live map with alert polygons.
- History and timeline view.
- Web Push and Telegram notifications.
- "My people" panel and shelter status card.
- Embed widget and broadcast view.
- PWA.
- Free.
- It does not replace official sirens. The disclaimer must stay visible.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and mark: מפה שקופה / ClearMap. The current logo mark stays as is.
- Alert polygon colors keep their current meanings (red, orange, purple and so on).
- The basemap tiles and their look stay.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real live data from Oref, through the backend.
- Do not fabricate user counts, press coverage, or endorsements.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity under stress beats decoration. The answer to "is it me?" comes first.
2. Never imply safety without an official source.
3. One glance should give state, scope, and freshness. Show when the data is stale.
4. Work equally in a dark shelter and in bright sun.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The audience includes elderly and stressed users. That calls for high contrast, legible sizes, and large touch targets.
